Cookie Policy

How RentEase uses cookies and similar storage technologies.

RentEase uses cookies and browser storage tools to support core site functionality, remember your preferences, and keep your account session working smoothly after sign-in.

Some storage is essential for the platform to operate, including tools that help authenticate users, maintain secure access to dashboards, and preserve settings such as whether you have already reviewed the cookie notice.

We may also use limited analytics or performance-related storage in the future to understand how the site is being used and improve reliability, navigation, and support. If that changes in a material way, this page should be updated to reflect it clearly.

You can control or remove cookies and site storage through your browser settings, although doing so may affect sign-in persistence and other core parts of the RentEase experience.
